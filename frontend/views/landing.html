Platform Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Designed with South African job seekers and businesses in mind — built for real-world conditions.

NQF Level Matching

Opportunities are tagged with NQF levels so applicants only see roles that match their qualifications, reducing wasted applications.

Real-Time Analytics

Providers get a live dashboard showing application volume, shortlisting rates, and placement success — exportable to CSV.

Application Notifications

Applicants receive instant notifications when their status changes — pending, shortlisted, hired, or rejected.

Reporting & Moderation

Applicants can flag suspicious listings. Admins review, investigate, and take action — keeping the platform trustworthy.

CV Management

Upload your CV once and it's attached to every application automatically. No more re-uploading files for each role.

Secure Authentication

Powered by Firebase — email/password and Google sign-in with role-based access control, keeping every account protected.
